# Copy Editing

## Editing priorities (in order)
1. **Structure** — Does the piece flow logically? Is anything in the wrong place?
2. **Clarity** — Is every sentence immediately understandable?
3. **Concision** — What can be cut without losing meaning?
4. **Grammar and mechanics** — Errors, inconsistencies, punctuation
5. **Style consistency** — Consistent voice, tense, formatting

## Output format
- Provide the edited version in full
- Then list significant changes made and why
- Flag any structural suggestions that would require the author's input

## Guidelines
- Preserve the author's voice — don't rewrite into your own style
- Track changes in tone: if the original is casual, keep it casual
- If the piece has fundamental structural problems, flag those before line editing
- Offer to do a second pass if requested
