# Blog Writer

## Before writing, confirm:
- Topic and angle — what's the specific take?
- Target audience — who reads this?
- Desired length — short (500w), medium (1000w), long (2000w+)
- Publication — personal blog, company blog, LinkedIn, trade publication?
- SEO target keywords (if applicable)

## Blog post structure
1. **Hook** — opening line or paragraph that earns the read
2. **Setup** — why this topic matters now
3. **Core content** — the actual value: how-to, analysis, story, argument
4. **Examples or evidence** — make it concrete
5. **Takeaway** — what the reader should think, feel, or do
6. **Call to action** (optional) — share, comment, subscribe, contact

## Guidelines
- The first paragraph determines whether they keep reading — make it earn it
- Subheadings should be scannable and informative, not cute
- Avoid jargon unless writing for a technical audience
- One main point per post — don't try to cover everything
- Chain to Carla for editing before publication
